# Pick Your AI volunteers

Help make AI architecture, compatibility, costs and evidence easier to understand. This hub supports community contributions to open research, documentation, education and software. The [documented direction](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/.github/blob/main/profile/README.md) is public benefit through transparent comparisons, learning and community participation; intended benefits are not measured outcomes or a legal qualification.

Pick Your AI intends to pursue US 501(c)(3) status. Incorporation, tax-exempt recognition, fiscal sponsorship and tax-deductible donation eligibility are **not established by this program**. This hub neither solicits donations nor authorizes financial or legal commitments.

[Browse the directory](docs/directory.md) · [Opportunities and priority needs](docs/opportunities.md) · [Apply on GitHub](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-volunteers/issues/new?template=application.yml) · [Operator Project](https://github.com/orgs/Pick-Your-AI/projects/14)

The application is public. No restricted intake has been installed. Share only your GitHub identity, interests, public-safe skills, approximate availability and support needs. **Recruiting capacity is currently unconfirmed.** An expression of interest is welcome; it does not promise acceptance, a mentor or repository access. Decision automation stays disabled until the proposed authority policy has explicit approval.
