# Leadership and authority

Team leadership, repository maintenance, Project administration, organization governance, board membership and financial authority are separate. This program appoints none of those roles. The organization’s [decision #18](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/.github/issues/18) records specific team-management delegations and interim administrative coverage; it expressly distinguishes those from accepted day-to-day work.

A volunteer reviewer needs a written mandate: exact actions, receiving scope, fallback destinations, escalation approvers, availability verification date and approval reference. They must confirm capacity, explain decisions, record reasons, avoid conflicts, respect applicant consent and maintain a reachable handoff. A lead may coordinate work only within that mandate. Accepting an application does not authorize membership, admin privileges, spending, contracts, legal filings, public representation, outside contact or a board appointment.

Proposed v1 authority policy: designated reviewers may accept/decline or request information for assigned applications, propose consented referrals to approved destinations, and request escalation when unavailable. Designated escalation approvers may authorize reviewer handoff; no timeout grants approval. Empty routes use an explicitly approved organization fallback. This proposal remains **inactive** in data/authority.v1.json until a recorded organization decision supplies actual names, scopes, routes, availability and response periods. No person has been nominated merely because their GitHub role permits editing.
